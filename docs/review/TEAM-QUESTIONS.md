# TEAM QUESTIONS — where our plan differs from the spec site, or the site is unclear

The spec site (https://system-arch.vercel.app, snapshot in `docs/source/`) is the **source of truth**
(`docs/PROCESS.md` §2). Every row here is either:
- a place where our plan departs from a clear site statement, or
- a place where the site contradicts itself, is silent, or looks unbuildable.

**How it works:**
1. The user takes each row to the team.
2. The answer and date go in the last column.
3. `Status` becomes `confirmed` (we keep our proposal), `site-wins` (we follow the site as written)
   or `changed` (a new ruling).

Until then, the **Default** is what we build, and the row stays open.

`L####` means a line number in `docs/source/system-arch-llms-full.txt`. Client-facing product
questions also live in `VISION-ALIGNMENT.md` §7 (Q1–Q26). This file is the working list.

Rows T17–T66 come from the database site-alignment pass (2026-09-27), and T67–T72 from the review of
that design (DATABASE.md rev 3, same day). `docs/architecture/DATABASE.md` §11.4 maps each one to the
schema it affects.
